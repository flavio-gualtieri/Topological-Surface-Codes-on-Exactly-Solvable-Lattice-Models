Topological Surface Codes on Exactly Solvable Lattice Models

Flavio D. Gualtieri · MSc dissertation in Theoretical Physics · Department of Mathematics, King’s College London · November 2024

Topological codes protect quantum information from local noise by storing it in global, topological degrees of freedom of a lattice model. This dissertation studies two exactly solvable examples, the toric code and the color code, focusing on constructing logical operators and implementing fault-tolerant transversal gates. It develops the two-dimensional toric code: its stabilizer formalism and Hamiltonian, its four-fold degenerate ground state, its logical operators, and the fusion and braiding of its anyonic excitations, including boundaries and punctures. It then turns to color codes, which a local unitary transformation maps to decoupled copies of the toric code (two in two dimensions, three in three), and constructs transversal Hadamard, phase and C⊗d−1Z gates that reach beyond the Clifford group. An appendix introduces the algebraic topology used throughout.
